About

Experience
you can see

I'm Steve Currie — a London-based actor, commercial model, and voiceover artist with over a decade of work across TV commercials, corporate productions, editorial campaigns, and broadcast drama.

At 48, I'm regularly cast in dad and family roles, which makes sense — I'm a father myself, and that kind of authenticity tends to read on camera. I bring a natural, grounded presence to every brief, and I'm comfortable in front of a lens in a way that comes from real experience rather than training alone. I'm also a working photographer, which means I understand how a shoot operates from both sides.

I've worked with some of the UK's most recognised brands across broadcast, digital, and print, and I've taken on international projects in mainland Europe and beyond. I take direction well, I'm reliable on set, and I don't make it complicated.
